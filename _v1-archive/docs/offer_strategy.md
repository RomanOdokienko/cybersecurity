# Offer Strategy (20/80)

## Objective
Use a limited free snapshot to trigger demo conversion without giving away full remediation value.

## Free Offer (20%)
Include:
1. SSL + redirect issue (if present).
2. One strong file leak/admin exposure signal.
3. Security headers grade with 1-2 clear misses.
4. One form/privacy compliance risk.
5. Short business impact summary.

Do not include:
- Full endpoint lists.
- Full OSINT evidence pack.
- Step-by-step remediation details.

## Demo (80%)
Include:
1. Full findings inventory and confidence.
2. Priority mapping by business impact.
3. Technical remediation roadmap (30/60/90 days).
4. Optional benchmark against peer clinics.

## Message Discipline
- No fear language inflation.
- Confirmed facts first.
- Hypotheses clearly marked.
