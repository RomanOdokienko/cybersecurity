# Project Context

Version: 2026-04-16

## 1. Project Idea
This project is an operating system for B2B lead generation in the clinic segment via passive security/compliance website audits.

We do not sell a "scan" as an end in itself. We sell a clear, evidence-based entry point for business dialogue: where a website has risks that are truly worth fixing.

## 2. Business Objective
The main business objective is to quickly and consistently classify websites into categories:
1. Strong and honest offer for demo.
2. Manual validation is required before offer.
3. Weak basis for an offer at this stage.

## 3. Product Model
20/80 model:
1. Free layer (20%): short, clear, and verifiable findings slice.
2. Paid layer (80%): full analysis, prioritization, and remediation roadmap.

## 4. What Counts as Client Value
1. Evidence-backed facts, not hypotheses.
2. Clear prioritization of what to fix first.
3. Translation of technical risk into business language: lead loss, trust, compliance.

## 5. Method Boundaries
1. Passive checks of public data only.
2. No exploitation, brute force, or port scanning.
3. One domain per audit run.

## 6. Project KPIs
1. Conversion "offer -> demo".
2. Conversion "demo -> upsell".
3. Share of false alarms (when a thesis falls apart during demo).
4. Analyst time per domain.
5. Share of sites with a clear next action after quick run.

## 7. Quality Management Principle
For MVP, a reproducible and explainable offer go/no-go decision is more important than maximum technical checklist coverage.
