# Acceptance Criteria

## Data Quality
- Each finding has source, timestamp, and confidence.
- No empty critical fields in normalized JSON.
- Scores are reproducible from inputs.

## Legal/Method Compliance
- Only passive/public-data collection methods.
- No disallowed action logged in evidence trail.

## Report Quality
- Free offer includes max 5 high-impact findings.
- Full report includes prioritization and remediation roadmap.
- Non-technical summary is understandable for business owner.

## Operational Quality
- Dashboard refreshes from latest normalized data.
- Queue status accurately reflects current state.
